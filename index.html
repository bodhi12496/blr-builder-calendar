Networking playbook

A simple routine for turning an event into relationships, learning and opportunities.

Before

  • Read the speaker and sponsor list. Pick three people you want to talk to and one question for each.
  • Try the host company's product for 15 minutes so you can ask a real question.
  • Prepare a one-line intro: what you're building or learning, and what you're curious about.
  • For hackathons, join the team-formation chat early. Teams form before the event starts.

During

  • Ask "What are you building, and what's the hardest part right now?" It surfaces motives and thought process fast.
  • Sponsor booths and mentor desks are staffed by the product team. Ask why they made a design choice.
  • Stay for the networking slot after talks. That's where most useful conversations happen.
  • Write a two-line note on each person right after you talk.

After

  • Connect within 24 hours, mentioning the specific thing you discussed.
  • Share a short write-up or your hackathon demo and tag the people who helped.
  • Go back to the same community next month. Familiar faces turn into friends and collaborators.
  • Offer something back: feedback on their product, a bug report, an intro.
